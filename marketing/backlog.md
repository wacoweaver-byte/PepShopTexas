## Rules for every change (read first)
1. **Research use only, everywhere.** No dosing, no reconstitution steps, no "for humans", no benefit or outcome claims, no before/after, no testimonials about effects. Describe compounds in lab terms only (structure, purity, what was tested). FDA's 2026 letters quoted ordinary product descriptions as evidence.
2. **Don't build traffic to GLP-1 products, bac water or calculators.** No content, titles or keywords targeting retatrutide, tirzepatide, semaglutide or their code names, bacteriostatic water, or "peptide calculator" / reconstitution / dosage terms. These are the exact items in the Aug 24, 2026 FDA letter to Tex Peptides (Dallas) and Eli Lilly's August 2026 suits against Texas sellers. Whether to keep selling them is Jimmie's call with an attorney; until then they stay out of search (noindex) and out of marketing.
3. **No PepKeep dose calculator on his site.** That's a standing rule.
4. **Allowed to publish without asking:** titles, meta descriptions, structured data, sitemap, robots, page speed, alt text, internal links, new content pages that follow rule 1, COA pages, and FAQ copy that follows rule 1. **Ask Mike first:** anything touching checkout, prices, payments, his database or server functions, emails to customers, or new products.
5. Test before publishing (load the page, check the console, check the HTML Google gets). Commit on a branch, merge to main, confirm the live version. Another Claude chat also publishes here, so always pull first.
6. Log each week's work in `marketing/efforts.json`: dated entries, plain words, short. Update `next`.
