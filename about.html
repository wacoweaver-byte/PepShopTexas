About Pep Shop Texas

Texas owned. Independently tested. Backed by a real person on the other end of the text thread.

Pep Shop Texas was created to provide quality peptides with proven quality and purity through independent third-party testing. Finding a peptide retailer you can trust is hard. We take on that challenge by being transparent and by making it easy to find what you need for independent research.

What we stand by

  • Independent testing. Batches go to third-party labs. Reports are posted on each product and on Lab reports, and any test is available on request.
  • Straight answers. Live stock, real prices, no games.
  • Fast, careful fulfillment. Shipped orders go to the post office the same night they're packed, or choose free local pickup.
  • Research use only. Every product is sold for laboratory research, not for human or veterinary use.
