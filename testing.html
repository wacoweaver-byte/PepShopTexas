Quality & verification

Peptide Testing

Pep Shop Texas uses independent analytical laboratories to verify the identity, purity, and content of research peptides.

Our Testing Standard

Independent testing is an important part of the Pep Shop Texas quality process. Samples are submitted to third-party laboratories for analytical testing before results are presented with the corresponding product.

Certificates of Analysis (COAs) are made available directly on the Product Details page for the applicable peptide so customers can review the testing documentation associated with that product.

Analytical Formulations Inc. (AFI)

Independent third-party analytical testing

Analytical Formulations Inc. is one of the independent laboratories Pep Shop Texas uses for peptide testing. AFI lists peptide analytical services including purity analysis and mass-spectrometry-based identity testing.

Laboratory testing reflects the submitted sample and should be reviewed together with the lot- or batch-specific COA shown on the product page.

Visit AFI Website

Freedom Diagnostics

Independent third-party analytical testing

Freedom Diagnostics Testing is another independent laboratory Pep Shop Texas uses for peptide testing. Freedom Diagnostics provides peptide purity and identity testing using HPLC and mass spectrometry, with Certificates of Analysis issued for submitted samples.

As with all analytical reports, the COA applies to the specific sample tested and should be matched to the product and lot information shown on Pep Shop Texas.

Visit Freedom Diagnostics

Where to Find the COA

When a COA is available for a peptide, it is loaded directly into that peptide's Product Details page. This keeps the testing documentation with the product information where it can be reviewed before purchase.
