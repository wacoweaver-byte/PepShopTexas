Quality & verification

Peptide testing

Pep Shop Texas uses independent analytical laboratories to verify the identity, purity, and content of research peptides.

Our testing standard

Independent testing is an important part of the Pep Shop Texas quality process. Samples are submitted to third-party laboratories for analytical testing before results are presented with the corresponding product.

Certificates of Analysis (COAs) are shown on the product page for each peptide, and every published report is listed below.

How to verify a certificate

  1. Open the product page and tap COA · Lab report. Note the lab, the report or certificate number, the test date and the lot.
  2. Gold Standard Analytics: products with a Gold Standard certificate show a "Verified by Gold Standard Analytics" box, read live from the lab. You can also open the lab's own copy at goldstandardanalytics.com/coa/ followed by the certificate number. If the lab ever retracts or can't confirm a certificate, our page shows "COA pending" instead.
  3. Freedom Diagnostics and AFI: the report names the lab, the sample and the date. To confirm a report, contact the lab with the report number shown on it.
  4. Check that the lot or batch on the report matches the one listed on the product page.

What the reports cover

Reports cover analytical results for the submitted sample: identity (mass spectrometry), purity (HPLC) and, where tested, content per vial. Pep Shop Texas does not give dosing or preparation guidance. All products are for laboratory research use only, not for human or veterinary use.

Laboratory testing reflects the submitted sample. Review each report together with the lot or batch shown on the product page.
