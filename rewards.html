PST Researcher Rewards

A four-level membership program designed to recognize returning Pep Shop Texas customers. Your level is based on cumulative qualifying merchandise purchases.

Benefits are not yet active. The rules below describe the planned program and may be updated before launch.

How spending counts

Only the actual qualifying merchandise amount paid after discounts counts toward tier progress. Shipping, taxes, complimentary products, cancelled orders, and refunded amounts are excluded. For example, a vial normally priced at $100 but purchased at 50% off contributes $50 toward membership, not $100. Eligible purchases accumulate toward the listed thresholds.

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose my free product on Silver?

No. Pep Shop Texas designates the complimentary product for Silver-tier offers. Gold and Diamond can choose from the eligible free-product selection associated with the offer.

Does every order automatically receive a BOGO reward?

No. Bronze members have no loyalty BOGO benefit. Higher-tier rewards depend on the promotion's terms and eligible product availability.

Do discounted products count at full price?

No. Only the net merchandise amount paid after eligible discounts counts toward membership.

Are benefits available today?

Not yet. This is an explanation of the planned program. Launch and account-based tier tracking will be announced separately.
