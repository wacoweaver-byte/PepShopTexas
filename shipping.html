When your order ships

Orders are processed once payment is confirmed and matched to your order number. Shipped orders are dropped off at the post office that night and travel by USPS Priority Mail, usually about 4 days to arrive. You'll get an email, and an app alert if you've turned them on, when payment is confirmed and when your order ships with tracking.

Packaging

Peptides ship as a dry powder, so no cold pack is needed. Every order goes out in protective packaging to keep vials safe in transit.
